# Trend Hunter — Top 10 leaderboard limit

- Each of the six leaderboard lists (Momentum gainers/losers, Volume Ratio gainers/losers, and % Change gainers/losers) now displays a maximum of 10 ranked stocks.
- Six stock rows remain visible on sufficiently tall screens; stocks 7–10 are accessible by scrolling the individual list.
- On small screens, fewer than six may fit without introducing overall page scroll.
- Counts and accessibility descriptions now use the 10-stock limit.
- The single-screen layout, Sector Flow visuals, authentication, ticker updates and scoring algorithms are unchanged.
